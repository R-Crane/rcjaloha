CardTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

CardTracker is a local-first iOS app. This policy is short because there isn't much to say: the app doesn't collect, transmit, sell, or share your data with anyone — including me.

What CardTracker stores

Card names, networks, annual fees, fee dates, points balances, and any notes you enter are stored using Apple's SwiftData framework, entirely on your device. There is no backend server and no account system, so this data never leaves your phone.

Notifications

CardTracker schedules local notifications (via Apple's UserNotifications framework) to remind you before a card's annual fee date. These are generated and delivered entirely on-device — no notification content is sent to or through any external server.

Analytics and tracking

CardTracker contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Data deletion

Since all data lives only on your device, deleting the app deletes all associated data. There is nothing stored elsewhere to request removal of.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional iCloud sync), this page will be updated first and the change will be reflected in the App Store listing's data collection disclosure.
